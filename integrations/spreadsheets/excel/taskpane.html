DC Hub for Excel

Data center & energy intelligence in Excel

Pull Data Center Power Index (DCPI) verdicts and scores, live grid demand, data center facility lists and state electricity rates straight into cells with =DCHUB functions.

Formulas work without a key and return previews. Paste a free key to open fuller answers. Every value comes from DC Hub (dchub.cloud); cite it with its as-of date.

Try these in any cell

  • =DCHUB.DCPI("phoenix")See at a glance whether a market's power supply says BUILD, CAUTION or AVOID.
  • =DCHUB.GRID("PJM")Track live grid demand in MW for an ISO next to your own numbers.
  • =DCHUB.FACILITIES("Ashburn",10)Get a ready-made table of facilities with operator, location and profile link.
